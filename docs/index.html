Every job has a hero.

TapJob is a wrist band that turns what you say at work into a paid bill. Double-tap, say what you did or what was ordered, raise your fist. The customer gets a payment page and the band turns green when they pay. No phone in your hand, no register.

Try it on your laptop See the five steps

Five steps, all from the wrist

  1. Double-tap the bandIt listens while you work. Its light is blue, so people nearby can see the microphone is on.
  2. Say it"Two double cheeseburgers, one fries, a diet soda and a shake." No prices.
  3. Double-tap againThe words become a ticket. Every price comes from your own price list.
  4. Raise your fistHold it up for a second, or double-tap. A Stripe invoice goes out with a payment page and a code to scan.
  5. Get paidThe customer pays on their own phone. The band turns green.

The model never touches the money

Prices are looked up, not guessed

Gemini only matches what you said to names on your price list. The price, the quantities and the total are computed in plain code, and the ticket marks every line that came from the list.

Any job, from one sentence

Type "I fix rockets on the launch pad". Gemini writes the card and a starter price list, and paints the hero. Paste or upload your own menu whenever you like.

A person confirms before a bill is sent

The ticket is shown and read back first. Only the raised fist, a double-tap or the Send button creates the invoice. Stripe test mode only in this build.

Try it

The band talks to a small program on your own laptop, so the live version runs there, not on this site. Without a band, the pretend one runs the whole loop in half a minute.
