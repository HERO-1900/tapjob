Every job has a hero.

TapJob is a wrist band that turns what you say at work into a paid bill. Double-tap, say what you did or what was ordered, raise your fist. The customer pays on their own phone and your band turns green.

Five steps, all from the wrist

  1. Double-tap the bandIt listens while you work. Its light turns blue, so people nearby can see the microphone is on.
  2. Say it"Two double cheeseburgers, one fries, a diet soda and a shake." No prices needed.
  3. Double-tap againThe words become a ticket, priced from your own price list, and it is read back to you.
  4. Raise your fistHold it up for a moment, or double-tap. A Stripe invoice goes out with a payment page and a code to scan.
  5. Get paidThe customer pays on their own phone. The band turns green and the ticket is stamped.

Pick your hero, or make your own

Each hero is a job with its own scene, its own way of billing and its own price list. Pick one and the whole page takes its colours. Not there? One sentence is enough.

Make your own hero. Type one sentence about your job. Gemini writes the card, the hero's name and a starter price list, then paints the figure with your tools, your scene and the band on a raised fist. Choose whether heroes are painted as women, men or either.

Raise your fist to send

The band knows which way it points. Teach the raised fist once; after that, holding it up while a ticket is waiting sends the bill. A double-tap still works.

Sent two bills today
💚

Paid means green

The bill is a Stripe invoice with a payment page and a code to scan. When the customer pays, the band turns green and the ticket gets its stamp.

Paid with Stripe's test card
↩️

Take it back

Nothing is sent until you confirm. If a bill went out by mistake, Discard voids the invoice at Stripe, so its payment page stops taking money.

⏱️

The band keeps time

For jobs that bill by the hour, start and stop are measured by the taps. Say the hours and they are used; say nothing and the band's clock bills them in quarter hours.

Bluetooth or a cable

The band talks to a small program on a laptop, over Bluetooth with no wire or over USB. No account, nothing to install on the band.

The model never touches the money

Prices are looked up, not guessed

Gemini only matches what you said to names on your price list. The price, the quantities and the total are computed in plain code.

A person confirms every bill

The ticket is shown and read back first. Only the raised fist, a double-tap or the Send button creates the invoice.

You can see where the money goes

The page names the Stripe account the bills are paid into. This build runs in Stripe's test mode only and refuses live keys.

What comes next

  • Sign in and connect your own StripeToday you paste your own Stripe key. Next: one sign-in, and the money goes straight to your account.
  • Let your AI sign you upTell your assistant what you do; it creates your hero, your price list and your account for you.
  • The phone appThe three screens above, on iPhone and Android, talking to the band directly.
  • The bill on the customer's phoneBy text message to a number you say out loud, or a pairing code the customer types.
  • Shake to undoA quick shake of the wrist takes the last ticket back, without touching a screen.
  • A shell for every brandThe band in your shop's colours, the hero in your uniform.
